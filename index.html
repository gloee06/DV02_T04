00 THE STORY

From national pattern to group detail

What can a decade
of data show us?

This visualisation follows non-fatal road traffic injuries that resulted in hospitalisation across Australian states and territories. It moves from changes over time to differences by sex, road-user group, age and hospital bed-days.

Use the charts to compare reported counts and inspect where data is unavailable. Counts describe the number of cases; they are not population-adjusted risk rates.

↳ READING THE DATA

Definitions · limits · source

Counts need
context.

The project uses Australian state and territory hospitalisation data for non-fatal road traffic injuries from 2011 to 2021. Figures are counts of cases and bed-days as reported in the source, not rates adjusted for population or travel exposure.

Some small counts are marked n.p. (“not for publication”) in the source. These values are unavailable, not zero. Question 4 therefore compares annual age-group totals only when both half-year values are published.

Source Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE), State and territory hospitalisation injury publication 2023. See the project’s data notes for processing details.
